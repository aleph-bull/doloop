Nothing matches that. Try fewer or shorter words, or one of these:

Getting started

Make an account, add what you're studying, and start your first session.

Creating an account and signing in

Where komogon.app

Go to komogon.app and choose "Create an account". You need an email and a password of at least 6 characters. If "Continue with Google" is shown, it signs you in to the account with the same email, or makes one.

  • "Forgot password?" on the sign-in card sends a reset code. Setting a new password signs out your other devices.
  • You stay signed in for 30 days.
  • Too many wrong tries in a row and you'll be asked to wait a few minutes.

Komodon is in an early test. The sign-in page says so: the person running it can see the accounts and study data saved there. Passwords are stored hashed and can't be read by anyone.

Your first screen: "Time to focus"

New accounts start on a simple screen while you have nothing in Tasks. "Got a lot due?" offers three ways in: "Paste from an AI chat" (a whole list at once), "Add your calendar" (a timetable or deadline calendar link), or "Add them yourself". As soon as there's a course or task, the full dashboard appears.

To see the full dashboard straight away, press "Show full dashboard", or turn off "Simplify the dashboard" in Settings › Pages.

Home, the dashboard

Where Home

  • Ready when you are: about how long today's plan is, with "Focus screen" and "Start a session". "Most pressing" folds out the top four things for today.
  • Your numbers: this week against your weekly goal ("Change goal"), the last 7 days, time studied all time, and both streaks.
  • Coming up (deadlines) beside Routines (today's, to tick or start).
  • Tasks: a row of task cards with their status, how much they need a day, and a play button.
  • Days focused: a grid of the days you studied, plus any trackers you add.
  • A daily card (a quote or a research tip), and now and then a tip about the app. Closing a tip asks if you want them off.

Your name

The dashboard asks "What should we call you?". It's used to say hello and shown on the leaderboard. Change it any time in Settings › Account (up to 60 characters). Without one, other people see "Someone".

Courses and tasks

Everything you're studying, as a table you can nest as deep as you like.

The Tasks page

Where Tasks

Courses sit at the top level, with units, assessments and tasks inside them. The columns are Name, Tags, Due, Time and Status. Each row has "Add inside", "Edit", a tick box and a play button to study it.

  • Types: Course, Assessment (quiz, exam), Task (homework, reading), or Other.
  • Estimated hours and a due date and time are what Komodon plans with. Leave the estimate empty if you don't know.
  • A parent without its own estimate adds up the estimates under it.
  • "Count activities as" (problems, pages, cards or your own word) lets the full review ask how many you did.
  • There's no delete. "Archive" takes an item out of view.

Pasting a whole term at once

Where Tasks › Paste a list

"Copy a prompt for your AI" gives you a prompt for ChatGPT or Claude. Give it your course outlines along with the prompt, then paste its answer back. Komodon also reads tables, spreadsheet columns and plain lines like "CSC148 lab 5 due Nov 6". You see everything, grouped by course, before you add it. Pasting the same list twice adds nothing new. Komodon itself never sends anything to an AI.

Status: on track, behind, critical

  • Behind: it needs more each day than you've been studying lately, and it's due soon.
  • Critical: finishing on time needs more each day than your "Comfortable minutes per day" (in Settings › Pace and streaks).
  • Overdue, No estimate, and Estimate met (you've studied as long as you estimated but haven't ticked it).

Ticking things off

Ticking a task marks everything unfinished under it as done too. Unticking it reopens only what was finished with it. Finished items fold into "Done" at the bottom of their course.

Tags, templates and moving courses

  • Tags: click a row's Tags cell to pick or make one (nine colours). The Tags card under the table renames, recolours or deletes them. Up to 20 tags on a task.
  • Templates: "Template for items under this" sets the type, estimate, due time and count for new items added inside that row.
  • Reordering: drag a course by the grip on its left, or use Alt + ↑/↓ on the grip.
  • Undo: Ctrl + Z (Cmd on a Mac) undoes edits, adds, ticks, archives, moves and tag changes.

Calendar

A week planner and a month of deadlines, with your classes on it.

What's on the calendar

Where Calendar (on a phone: Tasks › Calendar)

  • Planned blocks of study you've made, in a light tint.
  • Finished sessions, filled in with a check. A planned block you kept shows as a dashed outline behind the session.
  • Missed blocks in a faint colour. They're never flagged.
  • Classes and events from calendars you've added, in grey.
  • Due dates in a row at the top, with assessments flagged. Rest days are shaded.

Switch between "Week" and "Month". On a phone, Week shows one day at a time with a strip of the week above; flick sideways to change day.

Planning by hand

Drag across empty time to make a block (it snaps to 15 minutes), or click once for an hour. Drag a block to move it, even to another day, and drag its bottom edge to change its length. On a touch screen, hold for a moment before dragging. Ctrl + Z undoes a move.

"To plan", beside the week, lists what still needs time this week. Drag one onto the week, or click it to find a time.

Plan my week

"Plan my week" fits what still needs time into your free time, around your classes and rest days, with the nearest deadlines first. Suggestions are dashed: move them, keep or drop each one, or "Add all". Nothing is saved until you keep it.

Starting from a block

A planned block today that hasn't ended gets a play button. It opens the start dialog on that task, for the minutes left in the block.

Adding your timetable or course calendar

Where Settings › Calendar › Your calendars

"Add a calendar" and paste its link (webcal:// or an https link ending in .ics). With "Turn exams and deadlines into tasks" on, exams become assessments and deadlines become tasks, grouped under course codes like CSC108. Events become grey commitments that Plan my week plans around. Up to 10 calendars; each refreshes about every 15 minutes, or press "Refresh now".

  • Google Calendar: the calendar's settings, "Secret address in iCal format".
  • Outlook: Publish a calendar, then copy the ICS link.
  • iPhone / iCloud: share the calendar as a Public Calendar.
  • Moodle, Canvas, Quercus: look for Export calendar or Calendar feed.

The link is stored encrypted and never shown back. "Remove" takes its events away; tasks made from it stay.

Your plan in another calendar app

Where Settings › Calendar

"See your plan in another calendar app" gives you a private link to your planned blocks ("Copy", "Regenerate"), or "Download .ics once". Anyone with the link can see your planned blocks, so regenerate it if it gets out.

Calendar keys

← → move a week, a month, or a day on a phone. T today, W week, M month.

Routines and trackers

Things that repeat, and anything you want to count each day.

Routines

Where Routines (on a phone: Tasks › Routines)

A routine is something like daily reading or a weekly problem set. Give it a name, how long it takes (1 to 600 minutes), and how it repeats: daily, weekly or monthly, every so many, on certain days. Tick it off, or start it as a timed session and tick it at the end. Ticking shows the XP it earned.

  • "Due now" lists today plus anything unticked from the past week. "Clear missed" takes old ones off the list.
  • "Last four weeks" shows how often you kept to them.
  • Routine sessions count toward streaks and totals, but toward no task.
  • Routines can import repeating events from an .ics file with "Import .ics".

Trackers

Where Home › Days focused › + Tracker

Count anything daily: pages read, problems solved, flashcards, words written. Give it a unit and an optional daily goal, then log today with − and +. Deleting a tracker deletes its history.

Study plans

For anything without a syllabus: a language, an exam, interview prep.

Making a plan

Where Plans (on a phone: Tasks › Plans)

Say what you're learning, how many hours a week, and an optional target date. Add topics yourself ("Build it": topic, hours, resource, drills) or "Paste an outline" (there's a prompt to copy for an AI chat). A plan can add a short daily top-up routine too.

Drills and progress

"Log a drill" marks each question right, shaky or missed. Name the ones you missed and they come back on later days until you've got them. A topic counts as learned once your drills on it are reliably right. The plan page shows this week against your budget, a projected finish, what's up next and what's due back, and says whether you're "Ahead", "On track" or "Needs more time".

Grades

Your marks by class, and what you need on the rest.

Turning Grades on

Where Settings › Pages › Grades

Grades is off until you turn it on. Then it's a page of its own (on a phone: You › Grades).

Tracking a course

Each course is "By weight" (items with a weight and a score), "One mark", or "Not graded". Set its credits (1 for a full course, 0.5 for a half). Ungraded items don't count as zero. Set a target and each course tells you what you need on average from the rest to get there.

The grading scale is the University of Toronto's to start with. Change the bands, or set a goal GPA, in Settings › Grades.

Starting a session

Pick what to study, how you work, and for how long.

The start dialog

Where Home › Start a session, any play button, or the round Start button on a phone

  • What to study: open a course to pick a task inside it, or study a whole course or unit at once. Search finds tasks, courses and routines. "No task" runs a free session, and the minutes still count.
  • How you work: "Pomodoro" (25 minutes of work, a 5-minute break, a longer break every 4 cycles) or "Continuous" (straight through, no breaks).
  • How long: "Unlimited" (until you stop it), "For a while" (a number of minutes), or "Until a time".
  • Show the timer: hide it if a ticking clock stresses you out.

Komodon remembers the style and length you used last. If a set length would cut a Pomodoro block short, it offers a length that ends cleanly. Only one session runs at a time; starting another asks if you want to end the first (its time is kept).

Pomodoro lengths

Where Settings › Timer

Work 1 to 180 minutes (25 by default), short break 1 to 60 (5), long break 1 to 120 (15), and cycles before a long break 1 to 12 (4). "Auto-start breaks" and "Auto-start work after a break" are on by default. You can change these any time. A session that's already running keeps the lengths it started with.

During and after a session

Pausing, breaks, ending, and fixing a timer you forgot to stop.

While it runs

The dashboard's timer card shows the phase, the clock, what you're studying, and focused and break time so far. You can "Pause", "Skip break" (on a Pomodoro break), "+15 min" (on a continuous session with an end time), "Stop", hide the timer, or "Open the focus screen".

A session with an end time stops itself, with a minute's grace ("Ends in N s"). Work counts only up to the planned end.

Ending a session and the review

Stopping opens a short review, unless you've turned it off. "Quick" (the default) asks how focused you were ("Locked in", "A few drifts", "Scattered"), how ready you feel if the task is under an assessment, and whether to mark the task done. "Full" adds effort, how many problems or pages you did, whether it needs more time than planned, and a note. "Skip" costs nothing except the small review bonus.

Then the score sheet: focused time, the XP you earned and why, both streaks, your level, and any achievements. Closing it takes you to Social with the session ready to post. Change the review, or turn off "Go to Social to post it", in Settings › Timer › After each session.

Forgot to stop the timer

Where the score sheet's "Delete time", or Settings › Session history

"Delete time" trims time off the end of a session and takes back the XP that came with it. It shows where the session would now end before you confirm. A streak day that already counted stays counted.

A session that shouldn't count

Where the score sheet's "Don't count this session"

The session stays in your history, but gives no hours, streak day or XP. "Count this session" puts it back while the score sheet is open.

If your computer goes to sleep

The session keeps running. When you come back with a Komodon tab open, it asks whether you kept studying or stopped when the computer slept, and ends the session back then if you stopped.

The focus screen

Just the timer, big, over your background.

Opening it

Press "Focus screen" on the dashboard or "Open the focus screen" on the timer card. On a computer it opens in its own tab, so allow pop-ups for Komodon if your browser blocks it. On a phone it opens in the same tab. Closing it never stops the session.

To have it open every time, set "Open it when a session starts" to "Ask" or "Always" in Settings › Focus screen. New accounts start on "Never".

What's on it

The phase, the clock, "Course · Task", and a progress bar of what you've studied on the task against its estimate (or the length you chose), with this session's time beside it. The controls fade after a few seconds of stillness. The first tap only wakes the screen, so you can't hit Stop by accident.

Keys: Space pause, H hide the clock and task, Esc leave. The session keeps running when you leave.

The music visualizer

Where Settings › Focus screen, or the gear on the focus screen

Soft light behind the timer that moves with your music. It listens to your computer's audio through a tab or screen share with "share audio" ticked, never the microphone, and records nothing. You can tune its colour, intensity and the layers it draws (mist, waves, circles, sound bars and more).

Sounds

Where Settings › Sounds

Pick a sound for when work starts, when a break starts and when the session ends, or "Silent" for any of them. Volume is 50% to start. While the focus screen is open, it plays the sounds and the dashboard stays quiet, so each sound rings once.

Timelapse

A sped-up video of you studying, with the clocks and your task on it.

Turning it on

Where Settings › Timelapse › When a session starts

Choose "Ask", "Always record" or "Never" (new accounts start on "Never"). With "Ask", the focus screen asks "Record a timelapse?" once per session. You can also flip "Record this session" in the focus screen's gear menu.

On a computer

The camera records while you focus, and a pill at the top of the focus screen shows it's recording. Each time a recording starts, Komodon asks "Record your screen this time?" ("Just the camera" or "Record my screen"). The answer is for that session only, on purpose. If you stop sharing, "Share screen" in the pill picks it up again.

On a phone

Recording turns the focus screen into a camera screen with your timer on it. Prop your phone up and study. You can swap to the back camera, make the camera small, pause, or stop the session. The screen stays on while it records, and dims during work (70% by default). If the camera stops when the screen goes off, tap to start it again. Phones can't record the screen.

Live clips

While recording, "Live clip" (or L on a computer) films the camera at normal speed, for when there's something to show. Up to 30 seconds a clip and 60 seconds a recording. That part plays at real speed in the video.

Making the video

After the session, the "Your timelapse" card lets you trim it (drag either end), add a line of your own text to the video, and "Make the video". Then "Save the video" (a download on a computer; the share sheet on a phone, where "Save Video" puts it in Photos), "Share to Social", or "Change layout?" to redo the look.

  • It comes out about 20 to 40 seconds for a long session ("Auto"), or a set length or speed.
  • Breaks aren't filmed unless "Record breaks too" is on.
  • Chrome and Edge make an MP4. Some other browsers make a WebM, which Instagram and X won't take.

Layout and looks

Where Settings › Timelapse (a Phone and a Computer preset)

Start from a look ("Classic", "Big timer", "Headline", "Corner", "Clean"), then drag the pieces around: the screen, the current time, time elapsed, the task name, your text, the progress bar and the Komodon mark. The camera always fills the frame. Pick a shape (match the camera, 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 4:3). Nothing saves until you press "Save".

Posting it

"Share to Social" shows a preview first. Add a line, and turn off "Show how long I studied" to hide the minutes. "Post it on" Instagram, TikTok or X opens your phone's share menu, or on a computer downloads the video and opens the site. Komodon never posts for you and never connects to those accounts.

Your stills stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose "Share to Social". Closing the tab before saving loses the stills, so the focus screen asks first.

Analytics

Your hours, by the week, the month and all time. Only wins, never a guilt trip.

The tabs

Where Analytics (on a phone: You › Stats)

"Week", "Month", "All time" and "By task". Week and Month have arrows to look back. The top shows the period's study time against your weekly goal, your level, and a study personality once there's enough to go on (Night owl, Early bird, Deep diver and others). "Share my week" downloads a square picture of your week; nothing is uploaded.

The charts

  • Day by day with last week faintly behind, Where the time went by course, and When you study on a 24-hour clock.
  • Focus quality from your reviews, Session lengths, and Planned blocks kept.
  • Streaks and consistency, and a Trophy shelf of personal bests: biggest day, week and month, longest session, earliest start, latest finish and more.
  • Good to know: a few insights, like when you'll reach your next 100 hours at this pace.
  • By task: hours by course this week against last, how close your estimates were, and how ready you've felt over time.

Every chart has "Show as a table". Analytics counts days from midnight; only streaks use your day-start hour.

Streaks

A daily streak and a weekly one, both counting streak days.

What counts as a streak day

A day with at least one finished session of 15 focused minutes or more. Several shorter sessions don't add up: one session has to reach it. Change the minimum in Settings › Pace and streaks.

When a streak day starts

At 4 am by default, so studying at 1:30 am counts for the night before. A session counts for the day it started. Change it to anything from midnight to 8 am under "A new streak day starts at"; it can change once every 24 hours.

Daily and weekly streaks

  • Daily: streak days in a row. Today stays open until it ends, so a quiet morning never shows a broken streak. Milestones at 7, 14, 30, 60 and 100 days.
  • Weekly: Monday-to-Sunday weeks in a row with at least one streak day (or as many as you set under "Days a week for the weekly streak"). A week counts the moment it reaches its days.

Freezes and rest days

A streak freeze, bought with XP in the shop, covers one missed day of your daily streak on its own. Rest days, vacations and break days don't protect either streak: they shape your daily plan and pace only.

Where Settings › Pace and streaks › Rest days

Weekly rest days take effect next Monday. Vacations need 48 hours' notice and can be removed any time. You can make 3 changes a week.

XP, levels and rewards

Points for focused time, levels that unlock backgrounds, and rewards you set yourself.

How XP is earned

Mostly from focused time, with a little extra for filling in the review, staying focused, keeping to a planned block, being on pace, finishing within your estimate and keeping your streak going. The score sheet shows where each session's XP came from. Achievements and ticking routines pay too.

A session stopped very early earns no XP unless you marked the task done. The time still counts for streaks and progress.

Levels and achievements

Where the Level pill (the trophy) in the top bar or sidebar

Levels come quickly at first and spread out as you go. Hover the Level pill to see how far the next one is; click it for Achievements and its badges for things like your first session, streaks, hours, early mornings and long days.

The shop

  • Streak freeze: covers one missed study day automatically. You can hold two.
  • Break day: gives your daily plan a day off, picked ahead of time. It doesn't protect streaks.
  • Backgrounds: some unlock with levels, others are bought with XP. Every colour is free.

Your own rewards

"New reward" on Achievements: name it, pick an icon, and either "Spend XP" (a price and an optional cooldown) or "Finish a task" (it unlocks when the task is done). Up to 24 on the shelf. The lowest price you allow is "Minimum reward price (XP)" in Settings › Pace and streaks (150 to start).

Feed and posts

Share how a session went, see what friends got done.

The feed

Where Social › Feed

Your card and your friends on the left, "Live now", the box to post and everyone's posts in the middle, and the top five of the leaderboard on the right. Filter by "Everyone" or "Friends". You see your own and your friends' posts, and anyone else's who shares with everyone.

Posting

A post is always about one of your finished sessions ("Which session?"), and shows its minutes and "Course · Task". Add a line (up to 500 characters) and up to three photos and a video, shown one at a time: swipe, or use the arrows. Videos up to 40 MB, pictures up to 2 MB. If a file is too big, Komodon offers to compress it. "Friends only" keeps a post to your friends. Only you can edit or delete your posts (the ⋯ under them); editing changes the words only. A post with a video also has Trim video there, which cuts the video shorter.

  • Kudos: one thumbs-up per person; tap again to take it back.
  • Comments: up to 500 characters. You can delete your own comments, and any comment on your posts.
  • Replies: Reply on a comment starts yours with their @username. Put anyone's @username in a comment and they hear about it.
  • The bell: on your card beside the feed. Kudos and comments on your posts, mentions and nudges, newest first.

Friends and nudges

Who you study with.

Adding friends

Where the friends card beside the feed, or someone's profile

"Find people" searches by name. On a profile: "Add friend", then "Requested" until they answer. Requests wait under "Friend requests" ("Accept" or "Decline"). If someone asked you first, asking them back accepts. Declining lets them ask again later. Remove a friend from their profile's "Friends" chip.

Live now

Friends in a session right now show on the feed with a running clock and what they're on, first in your friends list, and on their profile. To hide yourself, turn off "Show friends when I'm studying" in Settings › Account › Social. People in your groups and jams still see you there.

Nudges

The bell next to a friend (or a group member) nudges them to study. You can nudge each person once every two hours. They see "A nudge to study" on their dashboard until they press "Got it", and nudges go away after a day.

Groups and jams

A lasting group of friends, or a few days of friendly competition.

Groups

Where Social › Groups

Start one with a name and share its six-letter code, or join a friend's under "Friends are in". A group shows the last seven days: your hours together, a line for each person, everyone ranked with their top subjects, and who's studying now. "Nudge" appears next to anyone under half the group's average. When the last person leaves, the group closes.

Jams

Where Social › Jams

A jam is a small game. Start one for "Today" (24 hours), "3 days" or "A week"; friends join, or anyone with its code. Whoever puts in the most focused time before it ends wins. Minutes count from the moment each person joins, so joining late gives no head start. The host can "End now"; anyone else can leave.

Leaderboard

All-time focused hours, with everyone or just friends.

The boards

Where Social › Leaderboard

"Global" or "Friends", the top 10 by all-time focused time, each with this week, days studied in the last 28 and their streak. Under the global board, "You and everyone" sets your week beside the average. Under the friends board, "Compare" puts your weeks beside your friends'.

Choose which boards count you under "Leaderboards" in Settings › Account › Social: "Global and friends", "Friends only" or "Neither".

Your profile

What other people see when they open you.

What's on it

Where the last row of the sidebar, your picture in the top bar, or You › Profile on a phone

Your picture, name, university, a line about you, when you joined, your friends, and your daily streak on your picture. Then the days you studied, your strongest days, your totals, your posts, your top badges and your peak study hours. The gear edits it.

Adding your university

Ontario universities, checked with your university email: enter it, then the 6-digit code it gets (codes last 15 minutes). One address can verify one account. The address itself is never shown.

Appearance

Make it calm, bright, cozy, or yours.

Themes, colour and background

Where Settings › Appearance

  • Themes: a whole look in one click, by tag: Minimalist, Nature, Cities, Night and Abstract. Dawn Skies is the one you start on.
  • Custom: the Themes tab for your own looks. "Save current look" names it to come back to (kept in this browser).
  • Mode: "Recommended" (light or dark to suit the background), "System", "Light" or "Dark".
  • Accent: Powder, Blush, Sage, Lilac, Peach, Lemon, or up to 8 colours of your own.
  • Background: 80+ photos and gradients by category, a solid colour, or your own pictures with Upload: the first is free, each one after 50 XP. They stay in the browser you add them in. Some unlock with levels or in the shop.
  • Texture: grain, dots or a grid on a solid colour or a plain gradient.

Panels and glass

Panels can be "Glass" (the default), "Opaque", "Custom colour" (any colour and opacity), or "Matte", which turns glass off everywhere. On a solid colour, Matte panels take their shade from it. Under Advanced, tune the glass's colour, weight, blur and colourfulness, or go back to the default glass.

Navigation and motion

"Navigation" switches a computer between the sidebar (the default) and a top bar. The sidebar opens on hover; "Keep the sidebar open" pins it. "Motion" is "System", "Reduce" or "Full". Phones always use the bottom bar.

Where every setting lives

Settings has a search box too. These are its sections, in order.

Account

Picture, name, about you, university, timezone, sign out. Social: who sees your posts, study totals on your profile, leaderboards, friend requests, showing friends when you're studying. Data and privacy: export, clear the offline copy, delete everything.

Pages

Turn Routines, Plans, Grades, Calendar and Social on or off. Dashboard: simplify the dashboard, the daily card (quotes, research tips, both or off), tips about the app.

Appearance

Saved looks, theme, accent, background, extension background, panels, navigation, motion, and Advanced (your own background, glass).

Timer

Pomodoro lengths, auto-start, show the timer by default. After each session: review (quick, full or off), going to Social to post it, phrases, confetti, badge toasts.

Focus screen and Timelapse

When the focus screen opens, and the visualizer. When to record, and the Phone and Computer presets: layout, length, frames per second, breaks, smoothing, evening out the light, dimming.

Sounds

On or off, volume, and a sound for work starting, a break starting and the session ending.

Pace and streaks

Weekly goal (5 hours to start), comfortable minutes per day, minimum minutes for a streak day, days a week for the weekly streak, when a streak day starts, minimum reward price. Rest days: weekly rest days and vacations.

Grades, Site blocking, Calendar, Session history

The grading scale and goal GPA. Sites to block during work (only the extension enforces this). Your calendars and your plan as a link. Every finished session, with "Delete time".

On a phone

Komodon is laid out for your phone, not shrunk from a computer.

The bottom bar

Home, Tasks, a round Start button in the middle (or "Back to your session" while one runs), Social and You. Tasks holds Calendar, Routines and Plans; You holds your profile, Stats, Grades and Settings, switched with the pills at the top of the page. Achievements opens from the Level pill.

Installing it like an app

  • iPhone: in Safari, Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android: in Chrome, the menu, then "Install app".
  • Computer: in Chrome or Edge, the install icon in the address bar.

It opens in its own window, and still opens with your last data when you're offline (changes wait until you're back online).

Chrome extension

In development. It isn't out yet.

What it will do

A timer remote in your toolbar (start, pause, skip a break, stop, tick routines, redeem rewards), minutes left on its icon, and site blocking: sites you list in Settings › Site blocking are blocked during work intervals only, and open again on breaks. Until it's released, the site blocking setting does nothing on its own.

Privacy and your data

Your data is yours. It's never sold, there are no ads, and it's used only to run Komodon.

Who sees what

  • Your profile (picture, name, university, about you, when you joined): anyone signed in. Your email is never shown.
  • Study totals on your profile: on to start; turn off "Study totals on your profile".
  • Posts: everyone signed in, or "Friends only".
  • Leaderboards: both, friends only, or neither.
  • Studying now: friends, unless you turn it off. Groups and jams you're in always see you there.
  • Groups, jams and Compare: the people in them see your minutes. Joining is the consent.

Where Settings › Account › Social

Exporting your data

Where Settings › Account › Data and privacy

"Export as CSV" (a zip of spreadsheets) or "Export as JSON". It includes your account, settings, tasks, sessions, planned blocks, achievements, routines, trackers, grades, plans, rewards and tags.

Deleting your account

"Delete all my data", type "delete", then "Delete everything". It deletes your account and everything in it, posts and their files included, and clears this device. It can't be undone, so export first if you want a copy.

Offline copy and signing out

Komodon keeps a copy of your data in your browser so it opens quickly and works offline. "Clear offline copy" removes it; signing out clears it too. Your data stays in your account either way.

Keyboard shortcuts

None of them fire while you're typing.

Anywhere

Questions

The things people ask most.

Does a session at 1 am count for yesterday's streak?

Yes, by default. A streak day starts at 4 am, so anything you start before then counts for the day before. Change it under "A new streak day starts at" in Settings › Pace and streaks (midnight to 8 am, once every 24 hours).

Why didn't my streak count today?

A streak day needs one finished session of at least 15 focused minutes. Shorter sessions don't add up, a session still running counts once it ends, and breaks aren't focus. Today stays open until it ends, so it updates as soon as a session qualifies.

Do rest days or vacations protect my streak?

No. They shape your daily plan and pace only. A missed day uses a streak freeze if you have one; otherwise the daily streak starts again.

How do streak freezes work?

Buy one with XP in the shop on Achievements; you can hold two. A freeze is used by itself on a missed day, and the dashboard tells you it covered your streak. Freezes are for the daily streak only.

What's the difference between Pomodoro and Continuous?

Pomodoro takes turns: work, then a break, with a longer break every few rounds (25 and 5 minutes, and 15 every 4, to start). Continuous runs straight through. Either can be unlimited, for a set while, or until a time.

Can I change the Pomodoro lengths mid-session?

You can change them any time in Settings › Timer, with no limits beyond 1 to 180 minutes of work. A session that's already running keeps the lengths it started with; the new ones apply next time.

Why did my session give no XP?

It was stopped very early and the task wasn't marked done. The time still counts toward your streak and progress.

I forgot to stop my timer. Can I fix it?

Yes. "Delete time" on the score sheet, or in Settings › Session history, trims time off the end of the session and takes back the XP that came with it.

What happens if my computer goes to sleep during a session?

The session keeps running. When you're back with a Komodon tab open, it asks whether you kept studying or stopped when the computer slept.

Why didn't the focus screen open when I started?

New accounts are set not to open it. Press "Focus screen" on the dashboard, or set "Open it when a session starts" to "Ask" or "Always" in Settings › Focus screen. On a computer it opens in a new tab, so allow pop-ups for Komodon.

Do my timelapse videos get uploaded?

No. The stills stay in your browser and the video is made on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose "Share to Social".

Does the timelapse record my screen?

Only on a computer, and only when you choose "Record my screen" as a recording starts. It asks every time. Phones never record the screen.

Can I use my iPhone's own Time-Lapse mode?

No. A website can't open the Camera app in Time-Lapse mode, so on a phone Komodon turns the focus screen into its own camera screen instead.

How do I post my timelapse on Instagram, TikTok or X?

After "Make the video", use "Post it on". On a phone it opens the share menu; on a computer the video downloads and the site opens in a new tab. Komodon never posts for you. X takes videos up to 2 min 20 s without Premium, and WebM videos can't go on Instagram or X.

Does the music visualizer use my microphone?

No. It listens to your computer's audio through a tab or screen share with "share audio" ticked, and doesn't record anything.

Who can see my study hours?

To start with: anyone signed in sees the totals on your profile, you're on the global and friends leaderboards, and friends see when you're studying. Turn each off in Settings › Account › Social. People in your groups and jams always see your minutes there.

Can people see my email?

No. Other people see only your picture, name and the profile fields you fill in. Komodon is in an early test, and the person running it can see accounts; the sign-in page says so.

What's a jam?

A small game with friends. Start one for a day, three days or a week, friends join (or anyone with its code), and whoever puts in the most focused time from when they joined wins.

What's the difference between a group and a jam?

A group lasts: a few friends who see each other's week, who's studying now, and can nudge. A jam is a one-off contest with an end.

How often can I nudge someone?

Once every two hours per person, for friends and people in your groups. Nudges show on their dashboard and go away after a day.

How do I add my class timetable or Moodle and Canvas deadlines?

Settings › Calendar › "Your calendars" › "Add a calendar", and paste the calendar's .ics or webcal link. Exams and deadlines can become tasks, and classes become times Plan my week works around.

Can I connect Google Calendar or Outlook directly?

Not yet. Add the calendar by its link instead: in Google Calendar that's the "Secret address in iCal format" in the calendar's settings.

Can I bring in a whole term's deadlines at once?

Yes. Tasks › "Paste a list". Copy the prompt, give it to ChatGPT or Claude with your course outlines, and paste its answer back. You see everything before it's added.

Why is a task "Critical" or "Behind"?

"Critical" means finishing on time needs more each day than your comfortable minutes per day. "Behind" means it needs more than you've been doing lately and it's due soon.

If I tick a course or unit done, what happens to the tasks inside?

Everything unfinished inside is marked done too. Unticking it reopens only what was finished with it, and Ctrl + Z puts everything back.

Where are Grades?

Off until you turn them on in Settings › Pages › "Grades".

Where are Achievements?

Click the Level pill (the trophy) in the top bar or sidebar. There's no tab for it.

How do I switch between the sidebar and a top bar?

Settings › Appearance › "Navigation". Phones always use the bottom bar.

Can I study without making courses or tasks?

Yes. Pick "No task" in the start dialog. The minutes still count toward your streak, level and analytics.

Is there a Chrome extension or a site blocker?

The extension is in development and not out yet. It's what will block sites during work; until then, the Site blocking setting does nothing on its own.

Can I install Komodon on my phone?

Yes, as a web app. On iPhone, Safari › Share › "Add to Home Screen"; on Android, Chrome › "Install app". There's no App Store app.

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Account › Data and privacy › "Delete all my data", type "delete", then "Delete everything". It can't be undone, so use "Export as CSV" or "Export as JSON" first if you want a copy.

Can I change my email or password?

Not from inside the app yet. If you can't get in, "Forgot password?" on the sign-in card sends a code to reset it.

Is Komodon free?

Yes. It's free to use and currently in alpha.
